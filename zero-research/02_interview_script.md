## Interview script v1

**Interviewer rules:** Ask about specific past behavior ("the last time…"), not hypotheticals. Never describe Zero before Section 4. Follow up with "Tell me more", "Why was that?" and "What happened next?". Don't agree or praise; stay neutral. Silence is fine.

### Section 1 — Warm-up and current habits (5 min, no product mention)
- **W1.** Tell me a bit about what you're studying for right now and what's riding on it.
- **W2.** Walk me through what a typical study day looked like yesterday or the last day you studied, from when you sat down to when you stopped.
- **W3.** What tools, apps, materials or people were involved in that day?

### Section 2 — Core problem exploration (riskiest assumptions first)
- **Q1 (A1, A7).** Think about the last time you stepped away from a subject for a few days. What happened when you came back to it? How did you figure out where you were?
- **Q2 (A1).** What, if anything, keeps track of what you've already learned, what you got wrong and what's next? How well does that work?
- **Q3 (A7, A5).** Tell me about the last time your study plan fell apart or you lost momentum. What caused it, and what brought you back, if anything did?
- **Q4 (A3).** Describe the last time you got stuck on a concept or problem. What did you do, step by step? How did you feel about how it went?
- **Q5 (A3).** When you use AI assistants for studying, what do you actually ask them for? What happens after you get the response?
- **Q6 (A4).** Walk me through how you move between your different study tools and materials in a session. What's that like?
- **Q7 (A6).** What materials are you studying from, and what do you do with them digitally, if anything? How do you feel about putting them into online tools?
- **Q8 (A5).** Has anyone or anything ever checked in on your studying: a person, group, app or service? How did that go?
- **Q9 (A7).** If you think about the hardest part of preparing for this exam or course, what is it? Why is that the hardest part?
- **Q10 (A2).** What have you spent money on for this exam or course so far, including courses, apps, tutors and subscriptions? How do you feel about each of those purchases now?

### Section 3 — Closing, willingness to pay and value anchoring
- **C1 (A2).** Of everything you've paid for so far, which felt most worth it and which felt least worth it, and why?
- **C2 (A2).** If something could take one problem you've described today completely off your plate, which problem would you pick? What would that be worth to you per month, and what would you stop paying for or stop doing to make room for it?

### Section 4 — Post-interview concept reaction (only after C2)
- **Z1.** [Read neutral one-line description: "An AI study system that works from your own materials, remembers your progress across sessions, guides you step by step rather than giving answers, and reaches out to keep you on track."] What's your honest reaction? What would make this useless to you?
