# Transcript — P5 刘佳怡 Liu Jiayi

*Synthetic interview. Persona: 19, 大二 mechanical engineering, 二本 in Henan, retaking CET-4 (last score 398) and taking 高数 (下). Interview date: late September, about three weeks into the semester, so not exam season.*

**W1. Tell me a bit about what you're studying for right now and what's riding on it.**

Um, right now? Honestly, nothing much. It's the start of the semester. I have 高数下 this term and I'm retaking CET-4 in December because I got 398 last time, so two points short. What's riding on it... mainly not 挂科. If I fail 高数 I have to take a 补考, and if that fails I have to 重修, which is embarrassing and my mom would find out. For CET-4, my parents ask about it every time they call, like "四级过了没有". Some jobs back home ask for it, so I do need to pass eventually. But I'm not doing anything for it right now, to be honest.

**W2. Walk me through what a typical study day looked like yesterday or the last day you studied, from when you sat down to when you stopped.**

Yesterday was not really a study day. I had class in the morning, and I took photos of the slides like always. In the afternoon I had to do the 高数 homework because it's due today. I did it in bed with my phone. I read the problems, didn't really get them, took a photo and put it into 豆包. Then I copied the steps onto paper. That took maybe forty minutes, with WeChat and 抖音 in between. If you mean real studying, the last time was June, the two weeks before finals. That was more like: wake up late, watch a B站 速成 video at 1.5x, do some past problems with a senior's notes, panic, sleep at 2 a.m.

**W3. What tools, apps, materials or people were involved in that day?**

豆包 for the homework, definitely. Sometimes 作业帮, because it has college textbook answers, but some of them are paywalled. My phone camera for the slides. The QQ class group, where the 学委 posts files and someone always shares last year's answers. In June it was B站, a senior's notes that my roommate got from her 老乡, and 百词斩 for CET words. I don't use my laptop much. It's mostly for WPS when we have a lab report.

**Q1. Think about the last time you stepped away from a subject for a few days. What happened when you came back to it? How did you figure out where you were?**

Hmm, I step away from everything for weeks, not days (laughs). With CET-4 vocab, I did 百词斩 for maybe ten days, then stopped for over a month. When I opened it again it said I had like 600 words to review, so I just reset the plan and started from the A words again. That's normal for me. I always restart from chapter 1 or from "abandon". I don't really figure out where I was. I just start over, or I check what the exam covers and start from there.

**Q2. What, if anything, keeps track of what you've already learned, what you got wrong and what's next? How well does that work?**

Nothing, really. 百词斩 tracks words, but I stop using it, so it doesn't matter. For 高数, the teacher's homework grades, I guess? I don't have a 错题本. My high school teacher made us keep one, but in university nobody checks, so no. What's next is decided by the exam. When finals are coming, the 学委 posts the 考试范围 in the QQ group and that's my plan. Does it work? Well, I passed 高数上 with a 63, so... kind of works?

**Q3. Tell me about the last time your study plan fell apart or you lost momentum. What caused it, and what brought you back, if anything did?**

I don't really make plans, so there's not much to fall apart. OK, last semester in March I said I'd do CET listening every day because listening killed me. I did it three days. Then my roommates' birthday, then 五一 holiday, and it just stopped. Nothing brought me back until the exam in June was close, and even then I didn't do listening, I did vocab because it felt easier. The thing that brings me back is always the date, when it's like two weeks away and I get scared. My mom asking also makes me feel guilty, but guilt doesn't make me study, it just makes me feel bad.

**Q4. Describe the last time you got stuck on a concept or problem. What did you do, step by step? How did you feel about how it went?**

Yesterday, a 多元函数 partial derivative problem. I read it, didn't know where to start, took a photo, put it in 豆包, and it gave me the whole solution. I copied it. Then the next problem was almost the same and I still couldn't do it by myself, so I asked 豆包 again. How did I feel... fine for the homework, honestly. It's done. But I know from last year that on the actual exam I can't use 豆包, and that's when I panic. Once I tried an app where the AI kept asking me "what do you think the first step is?" and I didn't know, that's why I was asking! It made me feel stupid so I closed it.

**Q5. When you use AI assistants for studying, what do you actually ask them for? What happens after you get the response?**

Answers. Like, "解这道题" plus a photo. Or for English, "translate this paragraph" or "write a 120-word essay about..." for practice (laughs). Sometimes I ask "can you explain it more simply" if I really don't get it, but mostly no. After I get the answer I copy it and submit. Sometimes 豆包 is wrong, and my roommate who is good at math says so, but usually it's fine for homework. I use 豆包 because it's free and it's already on my phone. My roommates all use it too.

**Q6. Walk me through how you move between your different study tools and materials in a session. What's that like?**

It's all on my phone, so I just switch apps. Photos, 豆包, QQ group files, B站. It's messy but I don't really think about it. Maybe the annoying part is finding the slide photos, because they're mixed with food photos and selfies in my album, so I scroll forever. And the QQ group files expire, so if I didn't download them in time I have to ask someone to send them again. But "too many tools" isn't a problem for me. I don't use that many tools.

**Q7. What materials are you studying from, and what do you do with them digitally, if anything? How do you feel about putting them into online tools?**

The textbook, which I barely open. Photos of the slides. The teacher sometimes puts the PPT in the QQ group. The senior's notes are photos too, kind of blurry. For CET, a 真题 book my mom bought me, and it's still pretty new, haha. Putting them into online tools is fine with me, I don't care about privacy for this. It's just 高数. But I don't have PDFs. It's all phone photos, like 200 of them. If an app wants me to upload a PDF or go to a computer to do it, I'm not going to do that.

**Q8. Has anyone or anything ever checked in on your studying: a person, group, app or service? How did that go?**

My mom, every phone call: "四级准备得怎么样了". I say "在准备了" (laughs). 百词斩 sends notifications, and I turned them off after a week because they were annoying. In June my roommates and I did a 图书馆打卡 thing for about four days, where we'd go to the library together, and that actually worked a bit because it was with friends. Then one of them got sick and we stopped. An app messaging me? I'd probably ignore it or turn it off, same as 百词斩. If it's my friends, that's different.

**Q9. If you think about the hardest part of preparing for this exam or course, what is it? Why is that the hardest part?**

Honestly? Starting before it's too late. I always know I should start earlier, and I never do. And then for 高数 specifically, I don't understand the basics, so when I cram, I'm memorizing problem types, not understanding. For CET it's listening. I can't catch anything, and I don't know how to practice it except just listening to the 真题 audio again and again, which is boring. But if I'm honest, the "hardest part" is that I don't care until two weeks before. During the semester there's always something more fun.

**Q10. What have you spent money on for this exam or course so far, including courses, apps, tutors and subscriptions? How do you feel about each of those purchases now?**

For myself, only ¥30 for one month of 百词斩 VIP, last December before CET. My mom paid for the 真题 book, maybe ¥40. I almost bought a 作业帮 membership once to see an answer, but it was like ¥25 for a month and I found the answer in the QQ group instead. Oh, and a friend shared her B站 大会员 with me, so that's free. How do I feel... the 百词斩 VIP was OK but I didn't use it for the full month, so half wasted. Everything else I get free, from seniors or groups. I don't pay for courses. My parents give me ¥1,200 a month and most of it goes to food.

**C1. Of everything you've paid for so far, which felt most worth it and which felt least worth it, and why?**

There's only really one, the 百词斩 VIP, so it's both (laughs). It was worth it a little because the week before the exam I did a lot of words, and I think vocab helped me get from like 370 to 398. Least worth it because I paid for a month and used maybe ten days. The 真题 book, my mom paid, and I only did two sets, so that's kind of a waste but not my money. The best "value" things were free: the B站 速成 videos and the senior's notes. Those basically saved me in 高数上.

**C2. If something could take one problem you've described today completely off your plate, which problem would you pick? What would that be worth to you per month, and what would you stop paying for or stop doing to make room for it?**

I'd pick passing 高数 without panicking in the last two weeks. Like, actually being able to do the problems on paper in the exam. Per month, honestly, I wouldn't pay monthly at all. During the semester it's ¥0, I wouldn't use it. If it really worked in the finals month, maybe ¥15, one time, for that month only. That's two cups of 蜜雪冰城, so I'd skip milk tea for a week. ¥39 is like eight cups of milk tea, no way, and ¥99 is crazy for a student. If it guaranteed I pass, maybe a bit more, but nobody can guarantee that. And if my roommates were all using a free version, I'd just use the free version.

**Z1. [Interviewer reads: "An AI study system that works from your own materials, remembers your progress across sessions, guides you step by step rather than giving answers, and reaches out to keep you on track."] What's your honest reaction? What would make this useless to you?**

Hmm. "Guides you step by step rather than giving answers" is the part I don't like. That's the app that made me feel stupid. When I'm stuck at midnight before homework is due, I want the answer. Maybe for exam prep step-by-step is better, I know that in my head, but I'd probably just go back to 豆包. "Remembers your progress" sounds nice, but I don't have progress to remember, I restart every time. "Reaches out" means notifications, and I'd turn them off. It's useless if it needs a PDF upload or a signup with lots of steps, if it's not free, or if it won't just give me the answer when I ask. If it could take my 200 messy slide photos and tell me "for the final, these are the problem types, do these ten", that I might actually use in December, and then I'd tell my whole dorm, if it's free.
