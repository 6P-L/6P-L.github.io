It's split across like four things. Anki tracks memorization, and honestly that part works really well, I trust the algorithm. **Q3. Tell me about the last time your study plan fell apart or you lost momentum. What caused it, and what brought you back, if anything did?**

End of August. I took a full-length, a Blueprint free one, and scored a 504, which was basically the same as my diagnostic, and I kind of spiraled. So I rebuilt the Sheet that Sunday around that. It wasn't a person or an app that pulled me back, it was more like I got scared enough and then found a plan that sounded credible.
